*User Stories*

Scenario 1: Initial Course Setup & Syllabus Parsing

Scenario: A student begins a semester and wishes to auto-organize their assignments' due dates from a cluttered syllabus without manually typing the information.
Assignment: Input or copy the course syllabus into the AI prompt parser to create an organized calendar of assignments, tests, and weekly readings.
Deadline: Friday of the first week of the semester (Add/Drop deadline).
 

Scenario 2: Study Plan for an Important Exam

Scenario: The user finds out that there is a midterm exam coming up in two weeks and requires a realistic plan of how to study.
Task: Generate a 10-day spaced repetition study schedule, indicate time blocks for studying (2h per day), and check micro-reviews created by the AI.
Deadline: 14 days before Exam Day (Study kickoff), 8:00 AM
 

Scenario 3: Rescheduling a Focus Block Due to Time Conflict

Scenario: An urgent meeting or doctor visit is scheduled during a 3-hour long focus block.
Task: Use the "Auto-reschedule" command to let the AI move other low-priority blocks to open slots further on the same week but not affect hard fixed deadlines.
Deadline: Same day of the time conflict, before 12:00 PM (noon).
 


Scenario 4: Assigning Sub-Tasks Among Team Members

Scenario: Students from the technical proposal group need to allocate deliverables and ensure timely delivery of individual parts of the project.
Task: List group members, create individual sub-deliverables (Literature Review -> Methodology -> Slide Deck), and set individual deadlines for each group member.
Deadline: 72 hours before final submission (internal peer-review freeze), 5:00 PM
